Dispatch & TransportWorking Prototype

InfraDispatch

Pickup points, vehicle fit, team handovers and approval details are often fragmented across messages and spreadsheets.

Target user
DMC transport coordinators, dispatch supervisors and destination operations teams
Inputs
Trip, pickup points, guest load, team, vehicle, costs and approvals
Output
Route logic, operational briefing, Google Maps links and print-ready manifest
Boundary
Browser-based prototype; inspect limitations below.
Product case study
Problem
Pickup points, vehicle fit, team handovers and approval details are often fragmented across messages and spreadsheets.
Operational context
Browser-hosted static MVP using operating rules and mock/reference data.
Product logic
Applies transparent rules to pickup pressure, zones, guest load, vehicle suitability, readiness and handover completeness.
What the prototype demonstrates
How an operational brief can be assembled consistently before dispatch.
Limitations
No live fleet telemetry, driver app, traffic API, authentication or production database.
Next development stage
Validated API integrations, role-based approvals and live dispatch status.

Commercial & QuotationWorking Prototype

InfraQuote

Tour quotations can hide cost classification, break-even and margin risk behind a final selling price.

Target user
DMC sales, product, costing and quotation-review teams
Inputs
Service assumptions, fixed/variable/conditional costs, guests, margin or markup, VAT and terms
Output
Cost worksheet, price, profit, break-even, readiness review and client quotation
Boundary
Browser-based prototype; inspect limitations below.
Product case study
Problem
Tour quotations can hide cost classification, break-even and margin risk behind a final selling price.
Operational context
Static browser MVP with user-entered assumptions and no live supplier rates.
Product logic
Uses documented commercial formulas and visible review thresholds.
What the prototype demonstrates
How pricing economics and operational readiness can be reviewed together.
Limitations
Not an accounting, tax, ERP or supplier-contract system; entered rates require user verification.
Next development stage
Controlled rate libraries, approvals and production quotation records.
