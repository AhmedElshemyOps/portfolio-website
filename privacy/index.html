Privacy & analytics

Useful measurement without personal guest profiling.

Current launch status: Google Analytics 4 is configured for this website. Analytics loads only after a visitor grants permission through the consent notice; visitors who decline are not tracked by Google Analytics.

What may be measured

With consent, the site may measure page views, approximate article reading depth, article navigation, project-demo visits, CV and credential-portfolio downloads, credential evidence views, AI prompt-copy actions, contact-link clicks and other clearly labelled interface interactions.

What is not intentionally sent

The implementation is designed not to send names, email addresses, certificate numbers, CV contents, copied prompt contents, form contents or other direct personal identifiers to Google Analytics. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled in the site configuration.

Your choice

When analytics is configured, visitors can choose “Allow analytics” or “Only necessary.” The preference is stored locally in the browser. Declining analytics does not prevent access to articles, projects, certificates or downloads.

External services

Links to LinkedIn, Credly, GitHub, certificate issuers and verification services open external websites governed by their own privacy terms. Certificate evidence displayed on this site was supplied for professional portfolio use.

Newsletter subscriptions

If the Tourism Operations Brief is configured, the email address submitted through a newsletter form is sent through a secure site endpoint to Brevo for double opt-in and subscriber management. The email address is not sent to Google Analytics. Brevo configuration remains inactive until Ahmed supplies the provider account, list, confirmation template and server-side API key.

Operations Lab scenarios

The applied Operations Lab tools calculate and generate outputs in the visitor's browser. Non-sensitive drafts may be stored in local browser storage and can be removed with each tool's Reset control. Entered passenger counts, costs, schedules, locations, process descriptions and other scenario values are not sent to the website server or Google Analytics. Visitors should use generic operating references and must not enter guest names, passport details, contact information, payment data or confidential supplier information.

Professional contact

If you contact Ahmed by email or through an external professional network, the information you choose to provide is used to respond to that communication. Do not send passport, payment or other unnecessary sensitive information.
