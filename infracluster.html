InfraCluster · UAE tourism location intelligence

Cluster large hotel pickups by area, timing, language, group size, vehicle fit and dispatch cost.

InfraCluster turns scattered hotel pickup and drop-off requests into practical operating clusters for UAE tours. It checks guest counts, infants, children, luggage, guide language, pickup windows, area spread, vehicle capacity and cost signals before recommending the cleanest dispatch split for InfraDispatch.

Create cluster planOpen InfraDispatch
Problem, solution, use case

Problem: Large UAE hotel pickups become messy when hotel areas, pickup timing, passenger mix, language needs and vehicle sizes are planned separately.

Solution: A static MVP that groups hotels into operational clusters, recommends vehicle splits, compares cost signals and prepares an InfraDispatch handoff.

Use case: DMC operations teams, MICE groups, city tours, airport movements, and dispatch supervisors preparing multi-hotel services.

The database is a static UAE tourism seed of 7,500+ rows across all seven emirates. It supports workflow testing, not a verified live hotel or attraction registry.
