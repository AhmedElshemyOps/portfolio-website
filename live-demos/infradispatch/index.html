InfraDispatch · Live Beta

Plan tourism pickups, team handovers and dispatch briefs in one clean flow.

InfraDispatch is a transport-team-first planning MVP for DMC and tour operations. It helps coordinators build pickup and drop-off plans, assign guide/driver/vehicle details, generate Google Maps route links, and prepare operational short messages without pushing the team through scattered notes.

Netherlands plannerAbu Dhabi planner How it works
Problem, solution, use case

Problem: Tourism pickup planning becomes risky when hotel sequence, guest counts, driver notes, guide notes, vehicle data and supervisor approval are handled separately.

Solution: A static MVP that turns pickup points, destination timing and team assignment into route logic, Google Maps links, operational messages, and a print-ready manifest.

Use case: DMC operations teams, transport coordinators, tour operators, guides, drivers and supervisors preparing city tours, airport movements and event services in the Netherlands and UAE.

    Who InfraDispatch Is For

    DMC and Travel and Tourism operations teams in the Netherlands and UAE

    DMCs & Tour Operators

    Hotel pickup sequencing, dispatch control, and transport planning software for daily operations.

    Transport Coordinators

    Driver, vehicle, and timing coordination with operational short communication outputs.

    Drivers & Tour Guides

    Clear pickup order, route links, notes, and handover-ready instructions.

    Core Workflow

    Create trip -> add pickups -> add destination -> assign team and vehicle -> generate dispatch plan -> export pickup sheet -> send team brief -> monitor status.

    Operating Focus

    Keep the MVP focused on practical planning: pickup order, route clarity, supervisor review, team messages, and manifest export.

    What this beta does now

    Supports route planning logic, operational short dispatch messages, team assignment, Google Maps links, and print-ready manifests.

    What this beta does not do yet

    No live traffic prediction engine, no backend user accounts, no cloud plan sync, and no server-side PDF automation yet.
