The quotation journey

One request. Two clear outputs.

  1. Request: capture or extract the enquiry and review the guest requirements.
  2. Build & price: reuse a tour, check dated supplier rates and compare service options.
  3. Review & share: resolve checks, save a version and prepare the client PDF or approved operations handover.

Problem

Operating gap

Tour quotations become risky when guest requirements, supplier costs, vehicle assumptions, tickets, VAT, margin and client terms live in separate calculations.

My role

Product and operations design

I structured the quotation journey, commercial calculations, internal controls and separation between cost review and client output.

Approach

Workflow response

A controlled quotation workflow that separates the internal cost view from client output while checking break-even logic, margin and readiness warnings.

Demonstrated outcome

What you can inspect

The demonstration makes pricing assumptions and warnings inspectable before a client-facing quotation is generated.

Use context

When it applies

Use it for new enquiries, custom itineraries, group quotations, supplier-price changes and every pre-approval review before a proposal is sent.

Inspectable outputs

Outputs you can inspect

  • Controlled cost worksheet
  • Margin and break-even review
  • Client-ready quotation and branded PDF
  • Reusable tour templates and company defaults
  • Dated supplier rates and three service options
  • Version comparisons, backups and approved InfraDispatch handovers
Transparent boundary

Amsterdam and Abu Dhabi workspaces

Choose your city to load its catalogue and cost basis: Amsterdam uses native EUR costs; Abu Dhabi retains its AED workflow. Amsterdam includes 25 attraction and route records, 15 dated public price references, four planning templates, age-based admission, service-date availability checks, explicit fare evidence for variable-price products and local operating prompts. Public rates are planning references, not confirmed bookings or supplier net agreements.

Current prototype scope

The public demo runs on GitHub Pages with browser-local drafts, templates, rates and version history. A separate shared-workspace backend is prepared but not connected. Sample rates still need supplier confirmation; accounting and live booking integrations are outside the demo.
