The quotation journey

One request. Two clear outputs.

  1. Capture the client request and guest requirements.
  2. Build the itinerary and review supplier assumptions.
  3. Check costs, pricing and readiness.
  4. Review the internal worksheet.
  5. Prepare a separate client quotation.

Problem

Operating gap

Tour quotations become risky when guest requirements, supplier costs, vehicle assumptions, tickets, VAT, margin and client terms live in separate calculations.

My role

Product and operations design

I structured the quotation journey, commercial calculations, internal controls and separation between cost review and client output.

Approach

Workflow response

A controlled quotation workflow that separates the internal cost view from client output while checking break-even logic, margin and readiness warnings.

Demonstrated outcome

What the prototype proves

The demonstration makes pricing assumptions and warnings inspectable before a client-facing quotation is generated.

Use context

When it applies

Use it for new enquiries, custom itineraries, group quotations, supplier-price changes and every pre-approval review before a proposal is sent.

Inspectable outputs

Outputs you can inspect

  • Controlled cost worksheet
  • Margin and break-even review
  • Client-ready quotation
