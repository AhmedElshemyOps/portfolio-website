Project case study · static MVP

InfraSky

Experience readiness

Problem

Operating gap

Stargazing and desert experiences can be oversold when moon phase, visibility, haze, access, camp readiness and guest comfort are considered too late or in isolation.

My role

Product and operations design

I designed the condition model, decision language, readiness scoring and the handover between product promise and field delivery.

Approach

Workflow response

A condition-aware planner that converts supplied conditions into a sell, watch, avoid or block decision with safer guest wording and field briefings.

Demonstrated outcome

What the prototype proves

The demonstration shows how a product team can make a bounded sell, watch, avoid or block decision from supplied operating conditions.

Use context

When it applies

Use it before selling, confirming or operating stargazing tours, astronomy programmes, desert dinners and other condition-sensitive outdoor products.

Inspectable outputs

What the demonstration produces.

  • Readiness decision
  • Guest-safe sales wording
  • Guide and field checklist
Transparent boundary

What it does not claim.

Static MVP using user-supplied conditions. It does not claim live weather, astronomy, access or safety data.
