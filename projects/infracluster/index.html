Research and product details
Problem

Operating gap

Large hotel pickup lists become difficult to operate when location, guest count, luggage, language, pickup windows and vehicle capacity are planned separately.

My role

Product and operations design

I translated hotel-pickup constraints into a reviewable clustering method, capacity checks and dispatch-ready output.

Approach

Workflow response

A clustering workflow that groups hotels into practical operating zones, flags capacity pressure and prepares a clean dispatch handoff to InfraDispatch.

Demonstrated outcome

What the prototype proves

The demonstration makes route grouping, capacity pressure and the reason for each proposed split visible to an operations reviewer.

Use context

When it applies

Use it for MICE groups, multi-hotel city tours, airport movements and any service where several pickup points must be consolidated into workable routes.

Inspectable outputs

What the demonstration produces.

  • Constraint-based clusters
  • Vehicle-fit warnings
  • Dispatch handover summary
Transparent boundary

What it does not claim.

Static MVP with illustrative clustering rules. It does not use live traffic, mapping optimisation or production booking data.
