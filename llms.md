## Projects

### InfraDispatch

Static live beta/demo for tourism transport dispatch planning. It supports pickup/drop-off sequencing, assignments, briefing outputs, Google Maps workflow links and print-ready operational manifests.

### InfraCluster

Static MVP for UAE hotel pickup and drop-off clustering. It supports a 7,500+ row static UAE tourism seed database, spreadsheet import, passenger mix review, timing-window grouping, guide-language matching, vehicle recommendations, estimated cost signals and JSON handoff outputs for InfraDispatch.

### InfraSky

Static UAE stargazing operations intelligence tool for season-aware planning, constellation visibility support, guest promise control, guide storytelling notes, and operational decision briefs.

### InfraQuote

Static quotation and costing tool for DMC and city-tour pricing workflows, including costing assumptions, margin review, guest inputs, transport needs, and client-ready quotation structure.
