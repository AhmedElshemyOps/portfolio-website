Editorial controls

  • Preserve protected manuscripts word for word.
  • Use clear headings, definitions and readable tables.
  • Keep dynamic facts distinguishable from durable frameworks.
  • Correct metadata and links without silently changing article arguments.

Responsible AI controls

  • Minimise personal and commercial data.
  • Label assumptions, gaps and uncertainty.
  • Verify live facts against approved sources.
  • Keep final operational authority with a qualified human.
