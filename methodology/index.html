Provenance and controls

Human-reviewed knowledge built from operating context.

The portfolio separates source material, interpretation, prototype logic and final professional accountability.

01

Experience first

Topics begin with a real tourism operating decision, handover, service risk or product question.

02

Sources visible

Where external facts or standards matter, the article records supporting sources and separates them from professional interpretation.

03

AI controlled

AI may support structure, classification or drafting, but approved inputs, privacy, verification and human sign-off remain required.

04

Claims bounded

Static demonstrations are labelled as demos. Credentials link to supplied evidence. Unverified performance claims are not invented.

Editorial controls

  • Preserve protected manuscripts word for word.
  • Use clear headings, definitions and readable tables.
  • Keep dynamic facts distinguishable from durable frameworks.
  • Correct metadata and links without silently changing article arguments.

Responsible AI controls

  • Minimise personal and commercial data.
  • Label assumptions, gaps and uncertainty.
  • Verify live facts against approved sources.
  • Keep final operational authority with a qualified human.
