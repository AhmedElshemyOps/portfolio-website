InfraSky · UAE stargazing operations intelligence

Turn sky conditions into a responsible tourism operating decision.

InfraSky helps DMCs, desert camps, guides and sales teams decide whether to sell, watch, avoid or block a UAE stargazing product. It turns the selected night into a realistic guest promise, guide story angle, driver/camp briefing and field checklist.

Run InfraSkyHow it works
Problem, solution, use case

Problem: Stargazing tours can be oversold when Moon phase, haze, access, camp lighting, guest comfort and guide capability are checked separately.

Solution: A static planning MVP that scores sky conditions, product fit and operating risk, then generates safe sales wording, guide notes, guest messages and field outputs.

Use case: DMCs, desert camps, stargazing guides, product teams and sales teams preparing condition-aware UAE astro-tourism experiences.

Operational honesty: InfraSky does not guarantee Milky Way, meteor or planet visibility. Forecast information is clearly separated from long-range planning estimates. Every confirmed operation must be re-checked 72 hours and 24 hours before service, with final on-site safety authority held by the operating team.
Public data policyForecasts are public/open-access only inside provider windows.

Weather and air-quality calls use Open-Meteo public APIs where available. Full-year planning dates are not live forecasts; they are astronomy, meteor-calendar and seasonal planning estimates.

Operating ruleManagement decision: Go Show, Watch, or No Show.

InfraSky separates sales confidence from field safety. Every confirmed service still needs 72-hour, 24-hour and on-site checks before the final guest promise.

      How it works

      One planning flow, designed for sales and delivery.

      InfraSky combines location operating data, product-specific astronomy logic, forecast availability, dust/haze indicators, comfort, transport feasibility and camp-lighting readiness. The result is a transparent decision—not a technical astronomy promise.

      1. Evaluate conditions

      Location darkness, access, weather, air quality, Moon brightness, seasonal sky context and guest comfort are separated into transparent scores.

      2. Match the product

      Milky Way photography, lunar telescope nights, family experiences and meteor campaigns use different weighting. A bright Moon can trigger a smarter product switch.

      3. Operate responsibly

      The dashboard converts the decision into times, sales wording, guide and driver briefs, field checks, escalation actions, annual planning and integration-ready payloads.

      Sky Intelligence

      From one selected night to a professional stargazing brief.

      InfraSky is not only a score. After the user enters the date, location and product, the MVP explains what the sky may feel like, which constellations are useful, when they appear or disappear, and what the guide should say on that specific night.

      01Read the nightMoon, darkness, haze, season and guest comfort.
      02Choose the promiseSell, watch, avoid, block or switch product.
      03Brief the fieldGuide story, driver timing, camp lighting and checklist.
      01

      Season and comfort

      Winter, spring, summer and autumn change guest comfort, haze risk, Milky Way potential and the safest product promise.

      02

      Moon and darkness

      The report separates Moon phase, illumination, estimated rise/set, astronomical darkness and product suitability.

      03

      Constellation window

      Each visible constellation card shows the best season, appears/disappears notes and the story angle for the guide.

      04

      Sky map logic

      The current MVP gives a text-based sky map and guide story plan. A future version can connect to a real sky-map API or planetarium engine.

      05

      Operation decision

      InfraSky converts the sky context into sell, watch, avoid or no-go guidance with risk notes and backup product suggestions.

      06

      Team outputs

      The result creates sales copy, guide briefing, guest WhatsApp, driver/camp briefing, SOP, checklist and export payloads.
