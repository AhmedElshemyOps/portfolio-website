Workplace improvement project

Travel Desk sales improvement

My 2025 Lean Six Sigma Black Belt project examined how strategic agent allocation could improve Travel Desk sales. The DMAIC approach combined cause analysis, statistical analysis and a control plan.

Proposed interventions included hotel allocation, multilingual sales materials, agent training, desk positioning, incentives and relevant offers.

Measured baseline
AED 8,059 median monthly agent sales.
Improvement target
AED 12,500 monthly agent sales.
Projected annual opportunity
AED 746,088 across 14 agents, conditional on achieving the target. This is a projected opportunity, not recorded realised revenue.
