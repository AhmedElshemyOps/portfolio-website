METHOD 01

Fact discipline

Observed data, derived metrics, assumptions, hypotheses and examples remain visibly separate. Public product listings never become invented private bookings.

METHOD 02

Human + AI

Humans frame the decision, collect and clean evidence, validate AI-assisted analysis and retain approval authority.

METHOD 03

Provenance

RAW → STAGING → CLEAN → ANALYTICAL → DECISION preserves the source, date, original value, transformation and reviewer.

METHOD 04

Stop is valid

The project is allowed to conclude DO NOT BUILD when visitor, commercial, operational or destination evidence fails.
