#Tour Quotation & Pricing Masterclass

A practical seven-article learning path for DMC operators, travel desk agents, tour coordinators and corporate tourism teams. The series explains how to build city tour quotations with stronger internal cost control, clearer client presentation and Abu Dhabi-based operational examples.

#What the series teaches

  • Cost structure and hidden-cost control
  • Markup, margin and break-even risk
  • VAT, ticket and gratuity awareness
  • Client-ready quotation standards

#How to use this series

Read the articles in order if you are learning quotation logic from the beginning. If you are a DMC supervisor or corporate travel manager, use the series as a training framework for travel desk agents, tour coordinators and junior product staff.

Internal view

Cost structure, supplier assumptions, operational buffers and margin control.

Operational view

Pickup logic, attraction timing, guide requirement and delivery risk.

Client view

Clear inclusions, exclusions, validity, price and service promise.

Professional standard: Every quotation should have an internal cost view and a separate client-facing quotation view. The client should receive clarity; the company should retain cost control.
