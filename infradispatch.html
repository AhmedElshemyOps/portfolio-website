    How it works

    From service details to a dispatch-ready plan.

    InfraDispatch keeps the workflow simple for the operator while the system handles sequencing, timing, vehicle logic, and message preparation in the background.

    01

    Add service details

    Enter pickup and drop-off locations, attraction timing, passengers, guide notes, and operational context.

    03

    Problem

    Pickup and drop-off planning is still too manual.

    In daily DMC operations, a pickup list is not just a list. It affects guest readiness, hotel access, driver timing, guide briefing, attraction arrival, and the final guest experience. When the information is spread across calls, WhatsApp messages, and manual notes, small mistakes can create delays and confusion.

    Solution

    One structured dispatch flow.

    InfraDispatch turns operational details into a clean flow: service inputs, pickup sequence, drop-off sequence, zoning control, and messages for the driver, guide, guest, and operations team.

    FAQ

    Simple answers for visitors and operators.

    Is InfraDispatch a booking system?

    No. This MVP is a dispatch planning assistant. It helps prepare route logic, vehicle recommendation, manifest, and messages before the service.

    Does it replace Google Maps?

    No. It keeps Google Maps as the direct route tool and adds an operations layer for sequencing, communication, and manifest readiness.

    Who is it for?

    DMC teams, tour operators, transport coordinators, guides, service delivery teams, and tourism operations supervisors.

    Can the database be expanded?

    Yes. Hidden admin mode allows locations to be added, edited, imported, exported, or reset without a backend.

    Important note

    InfraDispatch is an MVP planning assistant. Final route decisions should always be reviewed by the operations team based on live traffic, hotel instructions, supplier rules, guest requirements, and attraction access conditions.
